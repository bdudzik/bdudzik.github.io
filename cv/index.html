Professional Summary

Interdisciplinary researcher and educator working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, and cognitive psychology. Head of the Cognitive-Affective Technology (CAT) Lab at TU Delft, which studies context-sensitive computational models of how people think and feel.
